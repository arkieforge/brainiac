# Fixtures

`nadia/` is the fictional student every test, eval and screenshot uses: Nadia Karim, a BSc Computer Science
graduate of Northvale University applying to TUM's MSc Informatics. Nobody here is a real person. Keep the
files as people write them (comments, both quote styles, flow and block lists), because tests check that
Brainiac never reformats them.

`scans/` holds her documents as pictures, made for the OCR tests: a phone photo of a passport issued by
ICAO's specimen state Utopia (UTO) and the same passport scanned to PDF, a scanned transcript and a text
transcript with its university's wordmark, all printed to PDF by Chrome, and a made-up bank account number and
IBAN drawn the ways OCR must still read (on transparent pixels, cropped to the edges). They are drawn, not
photographed; nobody real is in them either.

`imports/` is what she brings in with `brainiac import`: a LinkedIn data export (a few of its CSVs, one of them
her connections, which is never read) and her CV as a PDF.
